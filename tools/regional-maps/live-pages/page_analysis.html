Sources and calculation

WorldPop 2025 R2025A constrained, UN-adjusted 1 km modelled counts. Uses the complete Primary roads layer already published in each cluster (© OpenStreetMap contributors), not a named project corridor. Roads outside the cluster are excluded. A 10 km buffer in a local azimuthal equidistant projection is dissolved and clipped to the cluster. For a national section, roads are first clipped to that country's territory within the cluster and the resulting buffer is clipped to the same territory. Overlapping road buffers count people once.

Counts are allocated by original-cell overlap area in Mollweide, assuming uniform population within each cell; the raster is not resampled. Shared overlay precision is 1 mm. OCHA COD-AB boundary gaps and overlaps remain unassigned. Missing raster cells stay excluded; valid-valued cell area is not a population coverage percentage. R2025A was released as an alpha product, and the constrained settlement model can omit or misallocate population. Web geometries are simplified by 20 m only for display.

10 km is a chosen analytical width, not an operational standard. This measures straight-line proximity, not travel access, all-season road access, community investment, economic impact or project beneficiaries. The country denominator is the population inside the cluster, not the full national population. This section has its own comparison selector and is independent of the geography controls above.

Source: © OpenStreetMap contributors / Geofabrik, 8 October 2026 (ODbL 1.0); existing OCHA COD-AB admin 2 boundaries. Percentages use summed junction counts. The 10 km path limit is a chosen analytical threshold.

Method, geography and interpretation

Junctions are shared OSM nodes incident to major and local roads with at least three statically accessible neighbours. Major roads include motorway, trunk, primary and their links. Directed motor-access paths exclude major roads and include a settlement-to-road snap of at most 250 m, using a 15 km network halo. Cities/towns are OSM place nodes; the alternative includes villages. Raw nodes are not merged into interchanges.

District and country summaries count junction locations inside the existing administrative boundaries and cluster. Routes may leave those areas or cross borders. Hexagons retain their original full-cell values when a district is selected. District percentages are calculated from junction counts, never from the average of hexagon percentages. Where a district has no junctions, its percentage is undefined. Districts differ in size; some Ethiopian admin 2 units are large zones. Clusters overlap and their counts should not be added as unique junctions.

Tiny incidental boundary overlaps covering less than 0.1% of a source admin 2 polygon and containing no junctions are omitted from the comparison, but retained and flagged in the source download. Original polygons assign the junctions; map outlines are simplified for display only. All junctions in this release have an unambiguous district assignment.

Conditional-access roads are excluded. Turn restrictions, live closures, surface condition and all-season passability are not modelled. The measure describes mapped local-road connectivity. It is not a population-access share or a verified measure of feeder-road use. Independent imagery or a road inventory with separate provenance is needed to assess missing mapping.
