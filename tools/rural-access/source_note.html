NASA SEDAC · Rural road access

SDG Indicator 9.1.1 Rural Access Index, 2023 release. CIESIN / NASA SEDAC estimates the share of rural population living within 2 km of an all-season road. The map shows the original source administrative units; the analysis page compares them and calculates summaries weighted by their rural populations.

Input dates: WorldPop 2020 population, GHSL 2015 urban/rural classification (R2019A), OpenStreetMap roads downloaded 31 March 2022, and GADM v3.6 boundaries. “2023” is the release date, not a common observation year.

Limitations: Values describe whole source units, including land outside the cluster boundary. Road tags indicate likely all-season status; proximity does not establish current passability or travel time. All 58 supplied records are retained, with 57 valid percentages. Original unknown names remain unchanged; the zero rural-population denominator for West Pokot “unknown 8” is shown as no data.
