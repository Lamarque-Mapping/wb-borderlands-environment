Source, weighting and limitations

CIESIN / NASA SEDAC, SDG Indicator 9.1.1 Rural Access Index, 2023 release. Each unit uses its rural population as the denominator. A group share is 100 × the sum of rural population within 2 km ÷ the sum of rural population across the valid selected units. It is not the unweighted mean of unit percentages. No area-overlap or supplied selection-tag weights are applied.

Inputs: WorldPop 2020 UN Adjusted population; GHSL 2015 urban/rural classification (R2019A); OSM roads downloaded 31 March 2022; GADM v3.6 source boundaries. All-season status is inferred from road tags. Geographic proximity does not establish current passability, travel time or affordable transport.

All 58 source records remain available; 57 have valid percentages. “unknown 8” in West Pokot has a zero rural-population denominator and is excluded from percentage summaries. Original unknown names are retained. Boundary size and source geography vary between countries; the results are not assigned to current OCHA districts or sub-districts. The 2023 release is not a common observation year.
