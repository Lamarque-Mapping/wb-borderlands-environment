Sources, geography and calculation

GUPPD v1 (2024) supplies source settlement footprints and names, with modelled GHSL R2023 population for 2020. Settlements are selected by source representative points inside a cluster; populations refer to the whole original footprint, which can extend beyond it.

GRDI v1 (2022) uses inputs from 2010–2020. Means and distributions include valid pixels only; the coverage figure is the approximate share of cluster area represented by those pixels. Missing building-footprint coverage is not evidence of no population or no deprivation.

GEHE supplies annual counts for 1983–2016. The historical mean requires all 34 years at each pixel. Area averages use fixed source-pixel locations and spherical grid-cell areas; edge inclusion follows pixel centres. They are approximate geographic summaries, not population-weighted exposure estimates. Source dates, limitations and licences appear in the table below.
